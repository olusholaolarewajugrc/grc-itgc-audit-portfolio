# Control Analysis – Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## Control Objective

To ensure that user accounts are protected through the use of multiple authentication factors before access is granted.

## Risk Addressed

Without MFA:

* Unauthorized users may gain access to systems.
* Compromised passwords may lead to account takeover.
* Sensitive information may be exposed.
* Privileged accounts may be abused.

## Control Design Review

The organization requires:

* MFA for user authentication.
* Additional verification factors beyond passwords.
* Continuous enforcement of authentication requirements.
* Monitoring of authentication activities.

## Auditor Assessment

The control design appears adequate to reduce the risk of unauthorized access resulting from compromised credentials.
