# Project 6 – Automated Backup & Encryption Review

## Objective

To evaluate whether backup processes and encryption controls are implemented and operating effectively to protect organizational data.

## Control Information

* Framework: ISO 27001
* Reference: A.8.13
* Control Owner: Infrastructure
* Frequency: Continuous
* Effectiveness: Partially Effective

## Audit Scope

The review focused on determining whether:

* Backups are performed regularly.
* Backup data is encrypted.
* Backup processes are documented.
* Evidence exists demonstrating backup effectiveness.

## Audit Result

PARTIALLY EFFECTIVE

Daily encrypted backups were implemented; however, evidence demonstrating backup validation and recovery testing was not available.
