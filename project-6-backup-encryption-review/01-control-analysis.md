
# Control Analysis – Automated Backup & Encryption

## Control Objective

To ensure organizational data is regularly backed up and protected through encryption.

## Risk Addressed

Without effective backup and encryption controls:

* Critical data may be lost.
* Systems may not be recoverable after an incident.
* Sensitive data may be exposed.
* Business operations may be disrupted.

## Control Design Review

The organization performs daily encrypted backups using AES-256 encryption.

## Auditor Assessment

The control design provides protection for backup data; however, additional evidence would be required to confirm that backups can be successfully restored and recovered when needed.
