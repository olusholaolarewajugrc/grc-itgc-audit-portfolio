
# Audit Finding

## Condition

Daily encrypted backups are performed; however, evidence of backup restoration testing was not available.

## Criteria

ISO 27001 requires organizations to ensure that information can be recovered when needed and that backup processes remain effective.

## Cause

Backup restoration testing was not documented or evidence was unavailable during review.

## Effect

The organization may be unable to confirm that backup data can be successfully restored during a system failure or disaster recovery event.

## Recommendation

Implement and document periodic backup restoration testing to validate backup integrity and recovery capability.
