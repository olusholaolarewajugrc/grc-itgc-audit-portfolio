# Audit Finding: Incomplete Privileged Access Review Evidence

## Finding Title

Incomplete Privileged Access Review Evidence

## Control

Access Review Process

## Control Area

Privileged Access Management

## Observation

The control was rated as partially effective. Based on the control description and status, the privileged access review process may not be consistently completed or fully documented.

## Risk

If privileged access reviews are incomplete or not properly documented, unauthorized or excessive privileged access may go undetected. This increases the risk of unauthorized changes, data compromise, and misuse of administrative privileges.

## Root Cause

The process may lack a formal documentation and approval workflow for privileged access reviews.

## Impact

Privileged users may retain access that is no longer required, and the organization may be unable to prove that access reviews were completed during an audit.

## Recommendation

The organization should formalize the privileged access review process by requiring:

- Complete privileged user listings
- Quarterly review completion
- Documented reviewer approval
- Retention of review evidence
- Follow-up actions for access removal

## Severity

High

## Auditor Conclusion

Control design exists, but evidence gaps indicate the control may not consistently operate effectively.
