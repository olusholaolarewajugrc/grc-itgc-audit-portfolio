# Evidence Notes — User Termination Control Review

## Objective

The objective of evidence review was to determine whether terminated employees had their access removed promptly and whether sufficient evidence existed to demonstrate control effectiveness.

---

## Expected Evidence

The following evidence would typically be requested during an ITGC review:

- HR termination records
- ServiceNow or JIRA termination tickets
- Active Directory account status reports
- Account disablement logs
- Email approval records
- Access removal reports
- User account listings

---

## Evidence Reviewed

For this simulated review, the following scenario was analyzed:

Employee: John Smith

Termination Date:
June 1

Observation:

HR processed the employee termination.

No documented workflow showed communication from HR to IT.

The employee account remained active for fourteen days after termination.

---

## Missing Evidence Identified

The following evidence was not available:

- HR-to-IT notification workflow
- Termination ticket
- Formal approval documentation
- Automated deprovisioning workflow evidence

---

## Auditor Assessment

The absence of documented communication and workflow evidence suggests the termination control may not be operating effectively.

Evidence indicated a process weakness rather than an isolated delay.

---

## Conclusion

The review identified a control gap in the termination process that may increase the risk of unauthorized access by former employees.
