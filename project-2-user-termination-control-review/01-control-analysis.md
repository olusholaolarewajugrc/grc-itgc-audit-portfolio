# Control Analysis — User Termination Control Review

## Control Being Tested

The organization should disable or remove system access for terminated employees promptly after employment ends.

---

## Control Objective

To prevent former employees from retaining access to company systems and sensitive information after termination.

---

## Why This Control Matters

Former employees with active accounts can create significant security risks.

Examples include:

- Unauthorized access to systems
- Theft of company information
- Data modification or deletion
- Insider threats
- Compliance violations

---

## Risk Scenario

Employee John Smith leaves the organization on June 1.

HR processes his termination.

However, IT is not informed.

John's Active Directory account remains active for two weeks.

During this period, he can still access email and internal systems.

---

## Potential Control Failure

There is no formal process ensuring HR communicates employee termination information to IT.

---

## Expected Evidence

Examples of evidence:

- HR termination records
- ServiceNow/JIRA termination tickets
- User account disablement logs
- Active Directory screenshots
- Email approval records
- Access removal reports

---

## Auditor Questions

- Was the employee terminated?
- Was IT notified?
- Was access removed?
- Was removal timely?
- Is evidence available?
