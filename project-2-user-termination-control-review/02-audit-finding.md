# Audit Finding — User Termination Control Review

## Finding Title

Delayed Deprovisioning of Terminated User Accounts

---

## Condition

During testing, terminated employee accounts were found to remain active after employee departure dates.

Sample testing identified one terminated employee account that remained active for fourteen days after termination.

---

## Criteria

Company policy requires employee access to be removed within 24 hours of termination.

---

## Cause

Communication between Human Resources and IT was not formally documented or consistently performed.

No automated termination workflow existed.

---

## Effect

Former employees may retain unauthorized access to systems and company information.

Potential risks include:

- Unauthorized access
- Data theft
- Insider threats
- Compliance violations

---

## Recommendation

Implement a formal HR-to-IT termination workflow.

Require documented evidence of account removal and periodic review of terminated accounts.

---

## Risk Rating

High
