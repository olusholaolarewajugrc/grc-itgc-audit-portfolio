# Control Analysis – Change Management

## Control Objective

To ensure that changes to systems, applications, and infrastructure are properly reviewed, tested, approved, and implemented.

## Risk Addressed

Unauthorized or poorly tested changes may:

- Cause service outages.
- Introduce security vulnerabilities.
- Result in data loss.
- Impact business operations.

## Control Design Review

The organization requires:

- Formal change requests.
- Management approval.
- Testing prior to deployment.
- Rollback procedures.
- Documentation retention.

## Auditor Assessment

The control design appears adequate to reduce risks associated with unauthorized or unsuccessful system changes.
