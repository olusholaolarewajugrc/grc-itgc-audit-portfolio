# Control Analysis — Privileged Access Review

## Control Being Tested

The organization should ensure privileged access is granted only to authorized personnel and reviewed periodically.

---

## Control Objective

To prevent excessive, inappropriate, or unauthorized privileged access to critical systems.

---

## Why This Control Matters

Privileged accounts have elevated permissions that can significantly impact systems and data.

Examples include:

- Domain Administrators
- System Administrators
- Database Administrators
- Cloud Administrators

---

## Risk Scenario

David Brown was granted Domain Administrator access to support a special infrastructure project.

The project ended two years ago.

No periodic access review was performed.

David still retains Domain Administrator privileges.

---

## Potential Control Failure

The organization does not perform periodic reviews of privileged accounts.

As a result, unnecessary privileged access may remain active.

---

## Expected Evidence

Examples of evidence include:

- Privileged user listings
- Access approval records
- Manager approvals
- Access review reports
- Role assignment documentation
- Recertification records

---

## Auditor Questions

- Who has privileged access?
- Why was access granted?
- Was access approved?
- Is access still required?
- Is evidence available?
