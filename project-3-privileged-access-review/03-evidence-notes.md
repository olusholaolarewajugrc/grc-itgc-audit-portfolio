# Evidence Notes — Privileged Access Review

## Objective

The objective of evidence review was to determine whether privileged access assignments were properly approved, justified, and periodically reviewed.

---

## Expected Evidence

The following evidence would typically be requested during a privileged access review:

- Privileged account listings
- Domain Administrator group membership reports
- Access request forms
- Management approval records
- Role assignment documentation
- Quarterly access review reports
- Recertification records

---

## Evidence Reviewed

For this simulated review, the following scenario was analyzed:

Employee: David Brown

Current Role:
Project Manager

Privileged Access:
Domain Administrator

Observation:

David Brown retained Domain Administrator privileges despite no longer performing system administration duties.

The privileged access was originally granted for a temporary infrastructure project that concluded two years ago.

---

## Missing Evidence Identified

The following evidence was not available:

- Quarterly privileged access review reports
- Access recertification records
- Current business justification
- Evidence of management reapproval

---

## Auditor Assessment

The absence of periodic review documentation suggests privileged access assignments may not be regularly evaluated.

Evidence indicates that excessive privileges may remain active beyond business necessity.

---

## Conclusion

The review identified a control weakness in the privileged access management process that increases the risk of unauthorized or inappropriate administrative activities.
